Stanby

Exploring delivery formats for entertainment research

Year
2025–2026
Role
Product development & demonstrations
Type
Entertainment data platform

Stanby is an entertainment data platform for campaign, event and concert-investment decisions. In an industry shaped by artistic judgement, existing data did not always translate into information clients could readily use. Clients wanted to understand which online campaigns could generate offline activity, where to hold events and how to approach concert investment.

I worked with Business Development on product development and demonstrations for US and European client opportunities. Those questions shaped the research, while clients’ ability and willingness to use it shaped the delivery format.

We started with dashboards, but many clients were unfamiliar with data tools and reluctant to spend time exploring the information themselves. A WhatsApp chatbot offered a way to ask questions and receive research through conversation, reducing the need to navigate a dashboard. However, clients already faced heavy work-message traffic, making them reluctant to use another service in WhatsApp and prompting us to reconsider the channel.
