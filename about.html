My perspective on AI Transformation

What is the human role in AI transformation?

AI has changed how teams move an idea forward. PMs can build prototypes, explore interfaces and give their teams something to try. The boundaries between product, design and engineering are becoming blurred. So where does our value lie?

Coding is fast.
Ideas are expensive.

Asking the right questions becomes more important:

What are we trying to improve?
How will we know it works?
What do we change when it falls short?

A convincing demo can start a conversation. It still needs to be tested against the work people actually need to do.

My Design Philosophy

I enjoy building, but I care more about whether people can use what I build.

I start by understanding their work and what gets in the way, then get hands-on with business and technical teams to work through the problem. Four questions help me weigh the options before committing to a solution.

01

People & adoption

Who needs this, and what would make it easier to use in their work?

02

Resources & delivery

What can we deliver and maintain with the time, budget and team available?

03

Business value

Is the change worth making, and what result would justify it?

04

Technical & data readiness

Do the data, access and systems support a reliable solution?
