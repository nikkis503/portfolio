Intro

I’m Nikki. I enjoy experimenting with new things and bringing ideas to life. There’s room in my work for both analytical thinking and playful ideas, and I don’t see them as opposites.

A strong visual can draw people in, but looking good is only part of it. What is it for? Would someone actually use it? Those questions always send me back to rethink an idea.

I have my own views, but I also want them challenged. I value questioning and Socrates’ approach of examining ideas through dialogue. Honest conversation matters to me. It helps me see things differently and find a better way forward.

My perspective on AI Transformation

What is the human role in AI transformation?

AI has changed how teams move an idea forward. PMs can build prototypes, explore interfaces and give their teams something to try. The boundaries between product, design and engineering are becoming blurred. So where does our value lie?

Coding is cheap.
Ideas are expensive.

Asking the right questions becomes more important:

What are we trying to improve?
How will we know it works?
What do we change when it falls short?

A convincing demo can start a conversation. It still needs to be tested against the work people actually need to do.
