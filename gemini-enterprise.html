Gemini Enterprise PoC

Exploring reusable skills and task-specific agent workflows

Year
2026
Role
Use-case exploration & reusable workflow prototyping
Platform
Gemini Enterprise · GCP

A proof of concept exploring how reusable skills and task-specific agents could support retail reporting and creative-asset preparation in Gemini Enterprise.

The work needed to demonstrate the platform within the required Google Cloud environment. My usual Claude Code and ChatGPT workflow could not replace that exploration, so the starting question became: “How far can I push what I already have?” Retail weekly updates provided a recurring reporting task, while creative-asset preparation offered a different type of work to explore.

For weekly retail updates, reporting guidance sits in a reusable skill. The agent workflow loads that skill and searches supporting retail data for the requested week. This keeps the instructions separate from the information needed for each report: the guidance remains reusable as the reporting period changes.

The prototypes brought these elements together in a skill library and task-specific workflows, making the relationship between reusable instructions, data retrieval and agent execution concrete within Gemini Enterprise.
