Database Migration

Year
2023–2025
Organisation
Lane Crawford
Role
Schema & reporting support
Platforms
Oracle → Databricks

Moving data from Oracle to Databricks would not, by itself, resolve differences in how departments defined and interpreted sales. My work at Lane Crawford focused on schema and reporting: establishing what departments needed to measure before deciding which data to carry forward.

Rather than review fields in isolation, I stepped back from the existing departmental reports to the shared core KPI, sales. Working through the factors affecting sales helped clarify the measures each department needed and where their definitions differed. This gave business and technical stakeholders a common starting point for reviewing reporting requirements.

Those discussions informed the data dictionary and key calculation formulas, linking the meaning of each measure to the data needed to calculate it. The agreed definitions then guided which fields to retain or retire and how to refine the reporting framework. Reporting needs provided the basis for schema decisions, rather than carrying conflicting interpretations into the new platform.

Clienteling Allocation Automation

Year
2023–2024
Organisation
Lane Crawford
Role
Segmentation support, UAT coordination and SQL investigation

A clienteling workflow to improve the efficiency of customer allocation and make sales follow-up visible. Previously, allocating high-value customers started with a SQL query that ran for over an hour. Excel files then passed through the retail office and store managers for checking before reaching sales associates.

These checks were part of getting customers to the right associates, but circulating spreadsheets created risks around customer information. Once the lists reached sales, the process offered no way to track whether follow-up happened. The priority was to make sales follow-up trackable, rather than simply generate customer lists more quickly.

Working with my manager, I helped define customer segmentation rules and coordinated UAT with retail and technical teams. The revised workflow assigns customers from the backend directly to sales associates through the sales portal, where associates record their follow-up. Removing three intermediate handoffs brought allocation and follow-up tracking into the same workflow, reducing reliance on passing customer lists between teams.
