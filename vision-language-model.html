AI Safety Reporting System

Risk detection with accountable follow-up

Year
2026
Role
Project management with a startup partner

An AI safety reporting system that opens risk reporting to individual workers. Previously, a small team identified and handled risks, making issues easy to miss. Wider participation brings more people into monitoring and reduces the team’s workload.

A vision-language model (VLM) identifies and classifies potential risks in images. The system then connects each finding to assigned follow-up and a reason for case closure, so detection leads to an accountable response. Working with a startup partner, my role covered requirements, development coordination and UAT, including detection accuracy requirements and SLA-based escalation to keep cases moving.

Decision flows and Storybook UI mockups helped business and technical teams review how the system should behave before implementation. I also used Claude Code for planning, reviewing generated outputs before engineering handoff.

Results: Entered its first enterprise-client pilot in September 2026 and has since rolled out to pilots with two enterprise clients. Enhancement and feature-development sprints continue.

Client identity, screens and implementation details are withheld for confidentiality.
