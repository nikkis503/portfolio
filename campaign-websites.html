underStan B2S Music Festival

Year
2025
Role
End-to-end website delivery
Development environment
Firebase Studio

A campaign website where university students could create and share their ideal festival lineup. Market research showed that self-made lineup posters were gaining popularity overseas. We brought the format to Hong Kong to build festival awareness through something students could personalise and share with their own social circles.

The poster gave students a way to express their own music tastes through the campaign. Choosing their own lineup made participation personal, while the finished image gave them something to share beyond the website.

I led requirements definition, UX and AI-assisted development with Firebase Studio. The flow centred on creating that poster: selecting or adding artists, choosing a style, and personalising the festival and creator names. Student-eligibility confirmation was incorporated into the experience, with the selected details carried through to the finished poster.

Results: Launched in July 2025 and attracted 6,000+ visitors, with participant posts showing personalised posters shared beyond the website.

Trial & Error Signed-Costume Auction

Year
2025
Role
Project management and data analysis
Development tool
Replit Agent

A branded website for a signed-costume auction, bringing artist selection and participation information together for fans. The site included payment functionality, which needed to remain stable as changes were made. Development ran from July to September 2025, ahead of the October launch.

My role covered stakeholder change requests, UAT coordination and campaign performance analysis, with Replit Agent supporting development. Many requests concerned the UI, but their effects could extend to payment functionality and system stability.

After launch, visual adjustments had to be assessed against the needs of a live service. The priority was deciding what could be improved without disrupting the core user flow. A requested change could improve the appearance of the site while introducing a risk to its operation, so changes with those implications were put on hold. Keeping the existing experience working took priority over further visual adjustments.

Results: Launched in October 2025; the campaign recorded 6M page views.
